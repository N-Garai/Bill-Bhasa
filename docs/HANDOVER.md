# Handover script — handing the app to its person (15 minutes)

1. **Install together (2 min).** Open the link on her phone → browser menu →
   *Add to Home Screen*. Show her the new icon.
2. **Say nothing (5 min).** Hand her today's real bill. Watch her tap the big
   camera button and listen — alone, unassisted. Note where she hesitates.
3. **Ask three questions on camera:**
   - *"Kya samajh aaya?"* (What did you understand?)
   - *"Pehle jaisa karna pada?"* (Did you need help like before?)
   - *"Kya badalna chahogi?"* (What should change?)
4. **Publish her verbatim words** — unedited, including criticism — at the top
   of the write-up, and fix the smallest friction she named before judging ends.
